I am the Alexander von Humboldt [German Research Chair of Mathematics and its Applications](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/research-chairs-at-aims-centres/german-research-chairs-in-portrait) at the [African Institute for Mathematical Sciences](https://aims.ac.za) (AIMS), in Cape Town, South Africa. I am also affiliated with the Department of Mathematical Sciences at [Stellenbosch University](http://www.sun.ac.za/english/pgstudies/Pages/Science/Mathematical-Sciences.aspx), and I am an associate of the [National Institute for Theoretical and Computational Sciences](https://nithecs.ac.za) (NITheCS). Before joining AIMS I spent just over two years at IBM Quantum, and before that five years as a post-doc in the group of [Jens Eisert](https://www.physik.fu-berlin.de/en/einrichtungen/ag/ag-eisert/index.html) at the Freie Universität Berlin.

I have broad research interests at the intersection of quantum computing, machine learning, cryptography and theoretical computer science. An up-to-date list of my publications can be found on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.co.za/citations?user=2zNzHR8AAAAJ&hl=en).

I am slowly building a research group at AIMS. If you are a prospective Ph.D. student or post-doc, please see the <a href="/opportunities/">opportunities</a> page for more information on open positions and relevant fellowships.

I can be contacted via rsweke@aims.ac.za
