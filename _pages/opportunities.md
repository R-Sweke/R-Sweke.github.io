AIMS offers an incredible Pan-African research environment, steps away from the beach in Muizenberg, Cape Town. I am slowly building a research group at AIMS, focused primarily on mathematical questions at the intersection of quantum computing, machine learning, cryptography and theoretical computer science. If you are potentially interested in either a Ph.D. or post-doc position, there are a variety of potential opportunities:

- Soon I will make an open-call for applications for a fully funded (3+1 year) Ph.D. position, to start in late 2025 or early 2026. Please check back soon for details!
- Soon I will make an open-call for applications for a fully funded (2 year) post-doc position, to start in early 2026. Please check back soon for details!

If you are based in South Africa:

- The [National Institute of Theoretical and Computational Sciences](https://nithecs.ac.za/) (NITheCS) has an [annual call](https://nithecs.ac.za/nithecs-bursaries/) for Ph.D. bursaries, and I will consider supporting applications of students as a prospective Ph.D. supervisor. Once the call for 2026 has been announced, I will post more details here of what I would require in order to support your application.
- The [South African Quantum Technology Initiative](https://saquti.org) (SAQuTI) has an annual call for Ph.D. bursaries, and again I will consider supporting applications as a prospective supervisor. Once the call for 2026 has been announced, I will post more details here.

If you are a post-doc based in Germany:

- I am an eligible host for Alexander von Humboldt [Feodor Lynen Research Fellowships](https://www.humboldt-foundation.de/en/apply/sponsorship-programmes/feodor-lynen-research-fellowship), which support research stays at AIMS of between 6 and 24 months.  If you would be interested to apply for such a fellowship, please contact me via email with a CV, and the subject line "AvH FL research fellowship".

Finally, if you are a Ph.D. student or post-doc with your own funding and would like to visit AIMS for a research visit, please reach out to me.
