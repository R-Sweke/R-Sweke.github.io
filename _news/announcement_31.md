Today our pre-print ["Distributed quantum property testing with quantum carrier pigeons"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08864) appeared on arXiv. This paper merges our old pre-print ["Distributed (quantum) testing with communication constraints"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05962) and Kenny Chen's work ["Distributed property testing with (quantum) carrier pigeons"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31753) in which he solved many of the open questions in our original work! Together we resolved one remaining open question, and so the full story is in this new pre-print :)
