I'm happy to announce that two of our submissions were accepted for short talks at [QTML2025](https://qtml2025.cqt.sg)! One, a joint submission with the group of Zoe Holmes on "kernel based dequantization of supervised QML" (based on [this](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.11647), [this](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15902) and [this](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23931) paper) and the other, a submission based on ["Interactive proofs for verifying (quantum) learning and testing"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23969).
